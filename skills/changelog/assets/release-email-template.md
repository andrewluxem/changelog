# Internal release email template

Use this asset when a changelog entry also needs an internal release announcement. Do not turn a planned item into a launch claim.

```text
Subject: [Released or Planned] [Release name] for [affected audience]

Release status: [Released, Partially released, Planned]
Release date: [Supplied date or Date needed]

What changed
[Two or three sentences in plain language. State what is available and to whom.]

Customer or user benefit
[Supported benefit and affected group. Write Evidence needed if the benefit is only assumed.]

Problem and evidence
[Problem addressed, with supplied feedback or data. Do not invent a figure or quotation.]

Experience
[How a person discovers, uses, or notices the change. Add supplied visuals or links.]

How success will be checked
| Signal | Baseline | Desired result or decision rule | Owner | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [signal] | [supplied or Baseline needed] | [supplied or desired result needed] | [Name or Owner needed] | [Date or Date needed] |

Known limitations
- [Supported limitation, impact, and current response]

Support and resources
- Help: [Supplied location or Link needed]
- Training or documentation: [Supplied location or Link needed]
- Feedback route: [Supplied route or Route needed]

What comes next
- [Next confirmed step]. Owner: [Name or Owner needed]. Due: [Date or Date needed].

Recognition
- [Person or team]: [Specific contribution supplied in the notes]

Frequently asked questions
Q: Who has access?
A: [Supported answer or Answer needed]

Q: Are there known limitations?
A: [Supported answer or Answer needed]

Q: Where should issues be reported?
A: [Supported answer or Answer needed]

Gaps to resolve before sending
- [Gap]. Owner: [Name or Owner needed]. Due: [Date or Date needed].
```
