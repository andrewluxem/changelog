# Changelog entry set template

Write for people affected by the change. Keep implementation detail only when it changes what they must do.

```text
Release or update: [Name]
Set status: [Released only, Planned only, Mixed, or Status needed]
Released-set date: [Supplied date or Date needed]
Audience: [Who is affected]
Prepared by: [Name or Owner needed]

Evidence gaps before publication
- [Missing ship status, date, user impact, availability, or support link]

New
- [Change title]
  Item status: [Released, Partially released, Planned, or Unknown]
  What changed: [Plain description]
  Why it matters: [Supported user consequence]
  Availability: [Who has it and when]
  Learn more: [Supplied link or Link needed]

Improved
- [Change title]
  Item status: [Released, Partially released, Planned, or Unknown]
  What changed: [Plain description]
  Why it matters: [Supported user consequence]
  Availability: [Who has it and when]

Fixed
- [Issue title]
  Item status: [Released, Partially released, Planned, or Unknown]
  What changed: [Observed behavior now corrected]
  Who was affected: [Supported scope or Scope unknown]
  Availability: [Who has the fix and when]

Removed or changed behavior
- [Change title]
  Item status: [Released, Partially released, Planned, or Unknown]
  What changed: [Plain description]
  Required user action: [Action or None]
  Effective date: [Supplied date or Date needed]

Planned or not released
- [Change title]
  Item status: [Planned, Partially released, or Unknown]
  What may change: [Plain description from supplied notes]
  Confirmed availability: [Not released, partial scope, or Unknown]
  Expected date: [Supplied planned date or Date needed]
  Evidence needed before moving into a released section: [Ship confirmation]

Known limitations
- [Supported limitation and who it affects]

Publication check
- Every item carries an item-level status, and planned or unknown work stays outside the released sections: [Yes or No]
- Dates and availability match the supplied notes: [Yes or No]
- Unsupported benefits, figures, and quotes removed: [Yes or No]
- Internal-only detail removed: [Yes or No]
- Support or migration action is clear: [Yes, No, Not applicable]

Open actions
- [Action]. Owner: [Name or Owner needed]. Due: [Date or Date needed].
```
