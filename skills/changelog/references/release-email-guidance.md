# Internal release email guidance

This reference merges the New Release Mail Template playbook into the changelog skill. The email turns the same verified release facts into an internal alignment artifact.

## Message structure

1. **Release status and date.** State what is available, to whom, and when.
2. **User benefit.** Explain the supported consequence for the affected group.
3. **Problem and evidence.** Show why the work was undertaken, using supplied feedback or data.
4. **Experience.** Describe how a person discovers, uses, or notices the change.
5. **Success checks.** Name signals, baselines, decision rules, owners, and review dates when supplied.
6. **Known limitations.** State what does not work yet, who is affected, and the current response.
7. **Support and resources.** Link only to supplied help, training, and feedback routes.
8. **Next confirmed step.** Give it an owner and due date.
9. **Recognition.** Credit specific contributions present in the notes.

## Frequently asked questions

At minimum, anticipate:

- Who has access?
- Are there known limitations?
- Where should issues be reported?
- Where can a team member learn enough to support users?

Add questions that the release facts make necessary. Do not answer from assumption.

## Draft versus send-ready

The message remains a draft when ship status, date, availability, user impact, limitations, support, or feedback routes are unresolved. Put those gaps at the end with owners and dates. Do not smooth them out of the prose.
